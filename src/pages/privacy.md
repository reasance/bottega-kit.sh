# Privacy

_Last updated: 2026-10-23._

This site uses **Umami** for analytics, and only once the site operator
enables it. No cookies, no fingerprinting, no cross-site tracking, no
advertising. You do not need to accept anything to read this site.

## Data controller

The site is operated by **Matteo Scurati**
([matteo.scurati@gmail.com](mailto:matteo.scurati@gmail.com)). For privacy
questions or to exercise your GDPR rights, write to that address.

## What is collected, and why

When you visit a page, a small script served by Umami reports an anonymous
event with: the URL of the page, the referring URL if any, your browser,
operating system and device type, your screen size, your browser language, and
your country. Umami does not set cookies, does not fingerprint, and does not
store IP addresses: the country is derived from the IP when the event arrives,
and visits are grouped by a salted hash that rotates, so a visitor cannot be
recognised over time or across sites.

The data is used to understand which pages are read and whether they load
well, so they can be improved. **Legal basis:** legitimate interest under
Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## Who processes it

**Umami Software, Inc.** (USA), which runs the analytics on **Umami Cloud**.
Its processing is described in
[Umami's privacy policy](https://umami.is/privacy).

**Cloudflare, Inc.** (USA) hosts this site on **Cloudflare Workers (Static
Assets)**, with EU edge locations serving EU traffic. As infrastructure
provider it processes minimal request metadata (IP, user agent, timestamp) for
security and rate limiting, as described in
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). It
runs no analytics for this site.

## What this site does not do

No cookies. No fingerprinting. No cross-site or cross-device tracking. No
advertising networks or third-party trackers. No data is sold, rented, or
shared.

## External links

External links to GitHub, npm, and similar may set their own cookies once you
click through. Their privacy policies apply there.

## Your rights

Under GDPR you may access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to the
processing of your personal data, and lodge a complaint with the
[Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.gpdp.it/). Write to
the email above. Aggregated analytics cannot be linked back to you, so erasure
of a specific past visit is technically infeasible.

## Opt out

Use a content blocker that blocks `cloud.umami.is`, or do not visit the site.

## Changes

If data collection changes, this page is updated and the date at the top
reflects the change.
